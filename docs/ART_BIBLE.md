# Art Bible

## Status

**LOCKED (2026-07-19) via captain lavish board WW-D0-art.**

**Style: hybrid A + B.**

- **Primary - A · Clay / soft toon 3D.** Cat bodies, enemies, props, and the world are rendered in a soft, hand-sculpted clay / toon look: rounded forms, gentle edges, matte stylized materials. Not photoreal. Not pixel.
- **Wardrobe - B · Outfit & accessories.** Layered on top of the clay bodies: outfits and accessories - **scarves, bags, explorer-kit props**. The B language governs *what characters wear and carry*, not the underlying body/world material.

**Rule of thumb:** bodies and world stay **clay/soft toon (A)**; wardrobe and carried props speak the **B outfit/accessory language**.

See [DESIGN_DECISIONS.md](DESIGN_DECISIONS.md) for the decision record.

## Direction detail

### Bodies & world (A - clay / soft toon)
- Rounded, sculpted silhouettes; soft-toon shading (stylized, matte, low-frequency detail).
- Materials: stylized PBR or toon ramp tuned to read as clay - avoid glossy photoreal and avoid flat pixel.
- Palette families (day hub / night / Rift) to be tuned as chapters land; keep them warm and readable against greybox until assets exist.

### Wardrobe & accessories (B - outfit / kit)
- Scarves, bags, explorer-kit props are the primary way characters read as distinct.
- Customization and identity flow through **swappable outfits/accessories**, not body restyling.
- Accessories should silhouette cleanly on the clay bodies at gameplay camera distance.

### Forbidden
- Photoreal materials, realistic fur/skin.
- Pure pixel / 2D-sprite look.
- Restyling body form per character in place of the outfit/accessory system.
