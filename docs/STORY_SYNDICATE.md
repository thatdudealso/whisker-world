# Story & Syndicate

Working narrative frame. **Final voice, villain design, and landing copy are captain-owned.**

## Rift frame

Something tore (or is tearing) between ordinary alleys and a stranger layer - the **Rift**. Cats feel it in whiskers and dreams. The syndicate either exploits the Rift, polices it, or both. Chapters should be able to touch Rift phenomena without requiring a full cosmic lore dump up front.
